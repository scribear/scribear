What this is for

Covers failure modes C1–C4 of the ScribeAR monitoring plan at the source, with no pipeline instrumentation: mic muted or wrong input selected (digital silence), level too low, clipping, and a rising room noise floor. Verify the calibration with a known tone — a full-scale sine must read -3.01 dBFS RMS (0.00 under AES17) and a -18 dBFS alignment tone must read -18, both within ±0.5 dB.
